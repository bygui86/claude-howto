
# Clone yourself in Claude

Built once. Runs forever. No excuses.

## 🔷 STEP 1: Write your about-me.md

Open a text file. Write 10-15 lines.

→ Who you are
→ What you do
→ How you communicate
→ What you never tolerate in your work

Drop it in your Cowork workspace folder.

This is Claude's first briefing.
Every task starts here.

## 🔷 STEP 2: Write your CLAUDE.md

Think of this as your standing orders.

"Read about-me.md before every task."
"Match my voice. Never sound generic."
"Ask questions before you execute anything."

Write them once.
They apply forever.

## 🔷 STEP 3: Build Projects, not chats

A Project is a persistent workspace.

→ Its own memory
→ Its own files
→ Its own instructions

One for content. One for ops. One for research.

Not a thread you scroll back through.
A system that already knows where you left off.

## 🔷 STEP 4: Connect your tools

Gmail. Google Drive. Google Calendar. Notion.

Connectors let Claude reach into the tools you already use.

Read your emails. Check your schedule. Pull your docs.

This is where Claude stops being reactive.
And starts being operational.

## 🔷 STEP 5: Install Skills

Skills = repeatable workflows. One command.

/weekly-report
/content-brief
/client-proposal

Type /skill-creator.
Claude builds it with you. Takes 10 minutes.
Saves hours, every week.

## 🔷 STEP 6: Schedule it

Pick your most repetitive task.
Set a frequency. Walk away.

Monday briefing. Weekly report. Inbox rules.

Cowork runs it automatically.
Even while you sleep.

Here's what you've built:

→ A version of Claude that knows your voice.
→ Knows your preferences.
→ Knows your workflows.

1. Save this post 📌(you'll come back to it)
2. Write your about-me.md today (15 minutes, one time)
3. Add one feature per day until the system runs itself
