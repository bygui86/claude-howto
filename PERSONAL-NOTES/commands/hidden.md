
# Hidden commands

## /ghost

→ When you want writing that sounds human instead of sounding like it was approved by a committee of robots.

## /roast

→ For those moments when you need honest feedback.
→ The kind that improves your work while quietly attacking your ego.

## /matrix

→ Turns a messy decision into a comparison table.
→ Perfect when you're choosing between tools, strategies, hires, or ideas.

## /steal

→ One of my favourites.
→ Instead of asking "what works?"
→ Ask Claude to reverse engineer WHY it works.
→ That's where the gold usually is.

## /brief

→ Because sometimes you need the answer.
→ Not the answer, a history lesson, three examples, and a motivational speech.

## /focus

→ For keeping Claude on task.
→ Which is useful because AI occasionally has the attention span of a Labrador that just spotted a squirrel.
